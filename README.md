# Ares Sentinel Website

Ares Sentinel is a professional consultancy website for a business that helps security companies modernise their CRM, operations, marketing, automation, and AI systems.

The website is designed to attract security company owners, directors, and operations managers who need better systems, clearer workflows, stronger lead management, and practical digital transformation support.

## About Ares Sentinel

Ares Sentinel works with security companies to understand how they currently operate, identify inefficiencies, and design bespoke CRM and business systems that support growth.

The company focuses on practical improvements across sales, operations, reporting, marketing, client management, and AI-enabled workflows.
