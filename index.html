The problem

Most security companies are running a modern operation on legacy admin.

You win contracts on response times, professionalism and reliability — then run the business on email chains, paper sign-in sheets, WhatsApp groups and a CRM built for estate agents. It works, until it doesn’t.

  • 01

    Leads slip through the cracks

    Enquiries land in shared inboxes. Quotes get written from scratch each time. Follow-ups depend on whoever remembers.

  • 02

    Sites and clients live in someone’s head

    Site instructions, access codes, client preferences and incident history sit in spreadsheets, notebooks or the operations manager’s memory.

  • 03

    Reporting takes days, not minutes

    Monthly reports for clients are stitched together by hand. Compliance evidence is scattered across three systems and a filing cabinet.

  • 04

    Off-the-shelf CRMs don’t fit

    Generic CRMs assume a sales pipeline, not patrol routes, guard rosters, site visits and incident logs. You bend the tool, or it bends your team.

  • 05

    Operations and sales don’t talk

    Sales sells the contract. Operations only sees it on day one. Margins shrink, mobilisation slips, clients notice.

  • 06

    AI is everywhere — except in your business

    Everyone is talking about automation. You don’t need a chatbot; you need fewer people typing the same incident report twice.

CRM for security companies

A CRM built around guards, sites and contracts — not just contacts.

Generic CRMs were designed for sales teams selling SaaS or insurance. Security businesses run on something more complicated: rotating guards, recurring patrols, layered client hierarchies, incident records, SIA compliance, key registers and assignment instructions.

We design and implement bespoke CRM for security businesses that mirror how your company actually operates — from first enquiry to live contract to renewal — so your team stops re-keying data and starts running the business.

  • Sites & assignments with location, access, hazards and SLAs

  • Client portals for incident reports, patrol logs and KPIs
  • Quotes, contracts and renewals generated from one record
  • Audit-ready reporting for ACS, ISO and client compliance
